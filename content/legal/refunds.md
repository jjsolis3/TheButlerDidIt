# Refund Policy

_Last updated: October 7, 2026_

We'd like everyone to be glad they paid for {{SiteName}}. If you weren't, and you haven't used what you paid for, you can have your money back.

## A full refund within {{RefundDays}} days, if unused

If you haven't started a game since a payment, you can have all of it back within {{RefundDays}} days of paying. This covers:

- **a new subscription**, monthly or yearly, counted from its first payment;
- **a renewal** of your subscription, counted from that renewal's payment;
- **a party pass**, counted from when you paid for it.

"Started a game" means a party of yours began while that payment was paying for your games: you began the evening of a mystery, or started the clock of an escape room. Creating a party and inviting guests doesn't count, and neither do the games of your free trial.

If you subscribe during your free trial, nothing is charged until the trial ends. There's nothing to refund before then: just cancel.

## How to ask

Email {{ContactEmail}} from the email address of your account within {{RefundDays}} days of the payment, and tell us which payment it is. We'll answer within 5 business days.

The refund goes back to the card you paid with. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

## What happens to your plan

A refunded subscription ends when we refund it, and your account goes back to the games you'd have without it. A refunded party pass simply runs out at its usual time.

## Cancelling instead

You can cancel a subscription at any time from Your account → Plans → Manage billing. Cancelling stops future payments, and you keep your games until the end of the period you've paid for. Cancelling doesn't refund the current period, unless it qualifies above.

## Anything else

After {{RefundDays}} days, or once you've started a game with it, a payment isn't refundable, except where the law says otherwise.

If we got something wrong, such as charging you twice, we'll put it right whenever you tell us. If the site was down or broken for your party, write to us and we'll look at it fairly.
